01 QUANTUM ENTROPY CIRCUIT

2-qubit circuit · Hadamard + CNOT · entangled seed generation
  • A pair of qubits is placed into superposition with a Hadamard gate, then entangled with a CNOT so measurement outcomes are correlated but not classically predictable.
  • The circuit is sampled repeatedly on the simulator (or hardware, when a QPU token is available) to accumulate a raw entropy bitstream.
  • Hybrid key generation feeds that bitstream into the classical KEM seed derivation step, so the trustworthiness of the seed — not raw bit volume — carries the security margin.
  • Since the circuit only needs a trustworthy seed rather than a wall of random bits, it stays intentionally minimal: 2 qubits are enough to trace the signal path end to end.
